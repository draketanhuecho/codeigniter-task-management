FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Vertex a real trading platform? +
This version is a front-end demonstration. Market numbers and order actions are simulated and do not execute real trades.
Can I use this as a dropshipping website? +
Yes as a front-end foundation. A production store would still need a backend, database, supplier integrations, authentication and a real payment provider.
Can products be added? +
Yes. The Add Product form stores demo products in browser localStorage so they remain available after refreshing the page.
Does the cart work? +
Yes. You can add products, view the cart, adjust quantities and open the checkout interface. Payment processing itself is not connected.
